04 / PAYOUT

Old threshold mock — not payout-ready

These historic test values do not confer holder eligibility or real CHOG. A redesigned vault must enforce current minted Raider Pass ownership at payment before any 50-CHOG transfer. In this old mock, each test box resolves to 5 CHOG (70%), 10 CHOG (25%) or 25 CHOG (5%) only after the owner-approved batch and oracle seed. Smaller on-chain credits carry forward in the same funded vault; the sponsor submits all redemptions and pays the gas. At 50 CHOG or more, the vault automatically sends the full balance to your locked address.

A normal campaign's end does not cash out sub-50 credit: it carries toward later funded campaigns. Only when the whole reward program is finally closed because no more campaigns will occur is the sponsor intended to settle remaining sub-50 on-chain credits promptly to the same locked addresses with owner-signed permits. Openings without an address remain pending until you confirm one; their 25-CHOG worst-case backing must stay ring-fenced indefinitely. Reviewed rules, funding, oracle operations and independent security review are required before public activation.
