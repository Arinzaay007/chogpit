Real CHOG prizes are not live.

GAME ONLY: The shooter pays no CHOG. Sample boxes and the browser-only sample Raider Pass never become entitlements. No real Raider Pass contract, public gas-sponsored mint, funded reward vault, live email login, holder verification or CHOG payout service exists.

Anyone can raid an existing Chog NFT arena and open sample boxes without owning either NFT or connecting a wallet. Chog NFTs remain the essential owner-built levels. Separately, a future free-to-claim CHOGPIT Raider Pass would be a real Monad NFT for players, with the issuer paying mint gas—not an official Chog NFT and not an arena-owner deed. The owner selected 40 total passes: a registered player would receive a user-controlled embedded wallet, then deliberately claim in a separate portal with issuer-paid mint gas. Registration alone would not mint an NFT. No real wallet provider, registration, claim portal or on-chain pass is available; the contract is drafted and locally tested, not deployed.

In a proposed future funded campaign, only the raider who opened a box could receive real CHOG, and only if they prove they currently hold a real minted Raider Pass before payout. The original arena's owner earns no such reward just because someone else raided it. A sample badge, an email link and a pasted address cannot prove ownership. An official Chog NFT alone would no longer meet this proposed payout rule.

The earlier email-account proposal allows up to two backed opening records per email across solo, private PvP and co-op, not two per person. Players would confirm a payout address after opening and before batching. How pass-unverified openings occupy the 40 backed slots is undecided; openings without a confirmed address would keep their original 25-CHOG backing reserved indefinitely. A free mint can be farmed with multiple wallets, so neither the pass nor email provides a one-person limit.

Proposed initial capacity is 1,000 CHOG for at most 40 worst-case 25-CHOG slots; possible awards are 5 CHOG (70%), 10 (25%) or 25 (5%). Sponsor-paid gas and payout of accumulated credit at 50 CHOG remain proposals. The email-only candidate and existing vault do not enforce Raider Pass ownership at payment and must not be activated. Exact UTC dates, funding, final rules and a compatible payout system are missing.
