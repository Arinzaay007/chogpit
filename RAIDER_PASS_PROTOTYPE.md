# CHOGPIT Raider Pass — claim-and-play prototype, not a mainnet mint

**19 October 2026 · prototype only.** This design adds a free **player pass** without replacing the official **Chog NFT arena deeds**. The Chog NFT still determines the pit, builder rights, wall route and off-chain battle scars. No NFT or wallet is needed to raid. The separate CHOGPIT Raider Pass is the proposed future **raider payout credential**, not a fighter, arena deed or official Chog NFT.

## What judges and players can try now

1. Open the **local game-only preview** or the updated Cloudflare ZIP **after an owner redeploy** (the public `chogpit.tech` build is older). Load any minted Chog NFT pit. Its trait/token identity and current on-chain owner still control the living arena.
2. Tap the lobby's **TRY SAMPLE RAIDER PASS · BROWSER ONLY** shortcut, then **TRY SAMPLE PASS**. The badge appears only in that browser's storage with **NO NFT / NO CHOG CREDIT** status. It does not call a blockchain or claim API. Refresh to see it remain labelled a sample; clear browser storage to remove it. It does not affect box openings, scores, room access or payouts.
3. Raid solo or in a private host-chosen PvP/co-op room **without connecting a wallet**. Hidden CHOG numbers are irrevocable examples. The **REAL NFT MINT NOT LIVE** button is disabled and `/api/pass/status` exposes `nftContract:null`, `realClaimEnabled:false`; `/api/pass/claim` is locked. `/api/campaign/status` and `/api/claims/status` are also locked.
4. Open the **separate local `raider-pass-portal/` preview** (`cd raider-pass-portal && npm ci && npm run dev`; Node 22+). Its **Privy** email signup and real NFT claim are OFF by default; `npm test` checks synthetically signed Privy identity tokens and a signed embedded-wallet/address binding, **not a live provider login**. An owner-issued **public** App ID, matching public identity verification key, exact allowed origin and local HMAC key can enable an **email-only Privy modal preview**. Privy then automatically creates a user-controlled EVM wallet without an external-wallet connection or registration signature. Dashboard settings must disable external wallets and enable signed linked accounts. No configuration enables minting. Privy publishes free **0–499 MAU**; the 40-pass cap is not a usage cap. See [`raider-pass-portal/README.md`](raider-pass-portal/README.md).
5. Using Node 22 in `/home/user/chogpit`, `npm ci && node tests/raider-pass.mjs` compiles the **draft real ERC-721** and executes gas-sponsored minting on a disposable EVM chain numbered 143. This is *not* a Monad mainnet transaction, NFT or CHOG payment.

## On-chain design draft, separate from the game-only ZIP

- [`contracts/ChogpitRaiderPassDraft.sol`](contracts/ChogpitRaiderPassDraft.sol) is an **unreviewed, undeployed** ERC-721 built with OpenZeppelin 4.9.6. The selected **40-pass maximum is hardcoded on-chain** in this draft; its constructor selects the issuer owner. Forty passes and forty proposed CHOG-backed box openings are separate ceilings. `mintWithConsent`/`mintBatchWithConsent` require the intended recipient's expiring EIP-712 signature bound to the specific contract, chain ID and one-use nonce. Only the issuer may submit the mint transaction and pay MON; the player does not pay for minting. Batches are capped at 20. The pass has self-contained on-chain art and standard NFT transfers. The contract cannot mint CHOG or modify an official Chog NFT.
- One lifetime mint per address is **not** one per person. People can create more wallets or acquire transferred passes. `balanceOf` or `ownerOf` of the deployed and independently verified **pass contract**, not a browser badge, would need to prove *current* ownership at the moment of a future CHOG payout.
- [`raider-pass.mjs`](raider-pass.mjs) constructs typed consent in isolated code. `REVIEW_BUILD.json` now records the draft pass ABI and compiled template hashes for independent comparison, **not a deployed code hash or audit**. There is **no public mint request database, relayer key, issuer signer, gas funding, real consent collection, owner-operated batch fulfilment, verified contract address or mainnet deployment**. No private keys or seed phrases belong in this workspace or chat. The existing prospective MON address is not proved to be controlled by the owner and is **not** a CHOG deposit address.

## Before anyone could receive a genuine free Monad NFT

1. Supply is **40** and **Privy’s Developer-tier path is selected**. The isolated portal uses the **email-only Privy modal**, not a whitelabel OTP flow, so `createOnLogin` can provision the user-controlled embedded wallet automatically without Connect Wallet or a signup signature. Its backend checks Privy’s ES256-signed email, DID and embedded EVM wallet; it needs the owner’s public App ID, **matching public identity verification key**, exact origin and local HMAC key. No live owner-app OTP/recovery or signed-wallet shape has been checked. Privy’s published free tier covers **0–499 monthly active users**, not unlimited signups; stop before paid usage. Set fair claim-access/anti-farming policy: 40 NFTs, one wallet and an email limit are **not** one-per-person controls. Registration creates a wallet **but not an NFT**. A deliberate later claim could mint only with independently audited project-gas sponsorship. A browser preview is not a claim queue. See [`EMBEDDED_WALLET_CLAIM_PLAN.md`](EMBEDDED_WALLET_CLAIM_PLAN.md).
2. In the owner's **own** wallet and infrastructure, deploy/verify the reviewed pass contract on Monad 143, set the owner/issuer intentionally, and prove its code/roles and sufficient MON for **issuer-paid** mint gas. There is no deployed contract address yet. Never paste seeds or keys into a document, website or chat.
3. Build and operate a secure **Privy email → automatically embedded wallet → deliberate claim** flow: at signup verify the provider-signed identity token’s email/DID/embedded address **without a wallet signature**; only on a later consented claim request the wallet’s expiring EIP-712 mint consent. Rate-limit and durably journal the claim, reconcile `everClaimed`/nonce and chain finality, let the issuer submit only reviewed batches, and mark **MINTED** only after a confirmed `ownerOf` read. Provide replay/failure/transfer/recovery handling. Do **not** enable a real claim-looking button without this service.

## Real CHOG is a second, separate launch

The owner-selected *future* rule is **only the box-opening raider who holds a genuine CHOGPIT Raider Pass before payment**; no pass is required to open a box, and owning the Chog arena alone does not grant that payout. A sample badge, signed mint consent, email link or old Chog NFT **does not** satisfy it. The draft threshold vault currently permits proof redemption without a pass-holder check, including its automatic ≥50-CHOG transfer. It cannot enforce this rule; redesign and review it before publishing any funded proof.

The proposed 1,000 CHOG / 40 worst-case-backed openings and two per email account remain **unfunded and unlaunched**. Free-pass farming and the reservation/expiry of *pass-unverified* openings under the 40-slot cap are unresolved; **unaddressed** openings separately retain their selected indefinite original-epoch 25-CHOG reserve. No age, location or person-verification policy has been chosen, and an exact UTC start/end has not been published. The owner confirmed CHOG has not been moved. Keep real reward routes disabled; see [`CAMPAIGN_RULES_DRAFT.md`](CAMPAIGN_RULES_DRAFT.md) and [`REWARD_LAUNCH_READINESS.md`](REWARD_LAUNCH_READINESS.md).
