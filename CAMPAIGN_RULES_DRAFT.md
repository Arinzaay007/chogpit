# CHOGPIT free-box CHOG campaign — proposed Raider Pass holder rules

**Draft v0.6 · 19 October 2026 · NOT IN EFFECT · no public CHOG payouts or real Raider Pass mint**

These are **proposed** terms reflecting the owner's latest choice: future real CHOG would go **only to the box-opening raider who holds a genuine, freely claimed CHOGPIT Raider Pass NFT on Monad before payout**. This **replaces** the earlier requirement to own an official Chog NFT to receive CHOG; Chog NFTs **remain essential arena-owner deeds** and are not replaced by the new player pass. Holding the pass is not required to start, raid or open a box. A local browser *sample pass* is not an NFT and never qualifies. Email sign-in for backed openings remains proposed, without person verification; the new pass is **not deployed**, and the email-only candidate and old vault cannot enforce holder-at-payment eligibility. Nobody has supplied final published rules or a launch date. Today's `chogpit.tech` boxes are non-redeemable samples; historical play and sample passes never become CHOG credits. This draft is not an eligibility determination or an instruction to send tokens.

## Free Raider Pass: issuance is separate from real CHOG

- The local **sample pass** is a browser-only illustration and has no on-chain contract, owner, token ID, monetary value or CHOG eligibility. The unreviewed [`ChogpitRaiderPassDraft.sol`](contracts/ChogpitRaiderPassDraft.sol) is a separate ERC-721 design: the wallet signs a non-spending, chain-and-contract-bound consent; the owner/issuer later pays gas to mint; there is no mint price, one mint per wallet lifetime and a **hard 40-pass total supply** in this unreviewed draft. Registering would create a **user-controlled embedded wallet**, not an NFT; the genuine pass would be minted only after a deliberate portal claim, with gas paid by the project's issuer. Guests remain wallet-free. **No embedded-wallet provider, real registration, deployment, issuer signer, funded gas sponsor, real claim intake or public mint has occurred.** A signed intent or displayed badge is not a minted NFT until a confirmed on-chain `ownerOf` read says so.
- Standard transfers would be possible; payment eligibility must use **current** pass ownership at the actual CHOG transfer, not the original minter or a cached wallet snapshot. One mint per wallet, two openings per email and even a limited supply do **not** prevent a person from creating more wallets/email accounts or obtaining passes from others. The **40-pass issuance cap is selected**, but distribution/anti-farming policy and whether a pass can support multiple email accounts are **[TBD]** before public minting or real CHOG. The 40 **pass NFTs** and the separate **40 CHOG-backed openings** are different limits; one pass is not a reward slot.

An email proves inbox access, **not** the later Raider Pass holder requirement. Abuse/remedy rules and lawful treatment of email-account opening limits plus holder-only payments in intended locations remain **[TBD]**.

## Address after opening; enduring obligations

1. On a successfully recorded opening, show its account-bound event ID as **PENDING REAL RAIDER PASS CHECK**, not as CHOG won. The award is not yet known. Wallet connection is **not** required before opening.
2. After opening and **before the first batch containing it is sealed/published**, the player enters the same Monad-compatible payout address twice and confirms it with a **fresh single-use email link sent to the registered inbox**, while signed in to that account. An inbox link does **not** prove NFT ownership or control of that destination. Wrong or inaccessible addresses may permanently lose CHOG; CHOGPIT never needs a seed phrase or private key.
3. **Before any CHOG is paid**, the opening raider must connect a wallet, sign a fresh, account- and payout-bound non-spending challenge, and have the service verify that this wallet **currently** holds at least one NFT from the **verified, owner-deployed CHOGPIT Raider Pass contract on Monad**. A pass token ID may be supplied so the service can recheck `ownerOf` against the signer. A wallet that merely pastes an address, displays a browser sample pass, once held a pass, owns only CHOG tokens, or holds only an official Chog arena deed does not meet this newly chosen payment condition. A separate email-confirmed payout address is **not** holder proof or proof it belongs to the opening raider. The design must either pay a raider-controlled address that itself passes the current-NFT check or prove and enforce a safe signed binding between a separately controlled payout address and that raider's holder wallet. **Which address must hold the NFT, how to bind both wallets and what happens if ownership changes before an automatic transfer are [TBD].** No deployed or local payout candidate enforces this.
4. The player may change the confirmed address through another fresh emailed challenge only **before the account's first batch is sealed**. Once batched, that destination is locked for this vault, including later funded epochs. There is no promised rebind after root publication.
5. **Addressless** openings remain pending without blocking addressed players' batches. Keep each such opening's **original-epoch 25-CHOG worst-case slot reserved indefinitely**, even after that epoch closes to new openings; do not silently discard or relabel it. **Raider-Pass-unverified** openings also cannot be paid, but whether their backing must remain indefinitely reserved is a *new, undecided* rule. Indefinite unverified slots could consume all 40 openings before actual holders qualify. Do not offer real openings until the reservation/expiry policy, recovery and financial responsibility are decided and published.

## Randomness, publication and payments

- For each ultimately qualified backed box, after a correct signed root is published and seeded, the possible award is **5 CHOG (70%)**, **10 CHOG (25%)** or **25 CHOG (5%)**. Odds are per opening. The owner signs an exact epoch/batch/root/entry-count/commitment permit; the sponsor pays to publish and request an oracle seed. The operator cannot substitute a browser-supplied recipient for the journaled, email-confirmed address. Publish auditable root, proofs, seed, events and outstanding-pending counts without disclosing raw emails; external journal checkpoints and proof retention remain **[TBD]**.
- The proposed sponsor would redeem **holder-eligible** proofs; credits accumulate to the permanently locked destination. Once that destination's accumulated credit reaches **50 CHOG**, the draft vault automatically transfers the **entire accrued balance**, with all gas paid by sponsor. **Critical incompatibility:** the existing unreviewed draft vault lets anyone redeem a published proof and does not check genuine Raider Pass ownership at redemption or threshold transfer. Therefore an off-chain-only check cannot guarantee the newly selected *holder-at-payout* rule. Revise and review the eligibility, batch/proof and contract enforcement before publishing any real proof. Sub-50 balances remain reserved across ordinary campaign endings and later **separately funded** campaigns; a normal campaign close is not final forfeiture.
- Only when the **entire reward program** finally ends because no more funded campaigns are expected, the sponsor would complete valid proofs and transfer remaining sub-50 credits to the same locked address under the owner-signed final-closure process, **only after enforcing the opening raider's genuine Raider Pass holder status at that transfer**. The final-close decision deadline and promptly measurable settlement schedule are **[TBD]**. Indefinite unaddressed-opening reserves still apply.
- A failed oracle, sponsor outage, deep chain reorg, invalid/unpublished proof, lost inbox or wrong destination has no complete live remedy today. Disclose and solve the handling, sponsor MON budget and long-term service commitments **before** inviting real-prize participation.
