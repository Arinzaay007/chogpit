# CHOGPIT reward readiness — 19 October 2026

## Decision: **NO-GO** for real CHOG

`https://chogpit.tech` is a sample-only game. The owner now intends **real CHOG only for the box-opening raider who holds a genuine CHOGPIT Raider Pass NFT before payout**; anyone may play and open sample boxes without holding one. Official Chog NFTs remain the owner-built arenas; they are **not** the new payout credential. The browser sample pass is not a minted NFT, and the free-to-player on-chain contract has **not** been deployed. This is a future proposal, **not a present claim**. Neither the email candidate nor the draft vault enforces pass-holder-only payment. No reviewed/deployed vault, funded capacity, real outbound email, active reward service or mainnet payout exists. The owner confirmed **no CHOG was moved**; 1,000 CHOG reportedly remains in the personal wallet. The prospective address `0x0d362Cf9443D6C2FF7Faa20C11111702e7099FC6` is **only for MON gas** and its control is unproved.

## Proposed scope, with an explicit fairness trade-off

- Free server-owned solo, private PvP **and host-selected private co-op** boxes; up to **40 potentially qualifying openings**, backed at **25 CHOG per opening** from an initial intended **1,000 CHOG** vault; proposed awards **5/10/25 CHOG at 70/25/5%**. No NFT purchase, CHOG balance, entry fee or player wallet is needed **to start or open**. Nonholders get sample-only play, not real CHOG.
- Real CHOG would go **only to the raider who opened the box** if that raider proves **current ownership of a real minted Raider Pass before payment**. Owning an official Chog arena deed or viewing a sample badge does not qualify; the owner of a pit raided by someone else gets no such reward. No wallet connection is needed at opening or sign-up. Fresh proof that the opening raider controls a **currently pass-holding wallet** would be needed before payout; the automatically provisioned embedded wallet could supply that proof without a Connect Wallet step. Neither email nor a pasted address proves holding.
- **Two opening records per email account**, across all modes. Single-use emailed login confirms inbox access, not holder or person status. No adult, residence, sanctions or unique-person verification was selected. A human can create multiple addresses and take several of the 40 slots; the same NFT may underpin multiple accounts. Appropriate public legal/age/location restrictions are unresolved.
- After opening, enter a destination twice, email re-verify before batch sealing and permanently lock the address once batched. **Addressless** openings remain pending with **25 CHOG original-epoch backing indefinitely** without holding other players up. Whether to retain or expire **pass-unverified** opening reservations remains [TBD]; unverified claims could occupy all 40 slots.
- Sponsor pays all MON and oracle costs. A *redesigned holder-enforcing* vault would transfer entire accumulated credit at **≥50 CHOG**, with sub-50 carried across ordinary funded campaigns and final sub-50 settlement after program closure **subject to holder verification at payment**. UTC start/end, final-close decision deadline and long-term support plan remain [TBD].

## Concrete launch blockers

1. The selected pass supply is **40**, separate from the 40 proposed CHOG-backed openings. **Privy** is selected for email-only modal login and **automatic, user-controlled EVM-wallet provisioning without Connect Wallet or a signup signature**. The owner’s Privy app, public identity verification key, signed email/embedded-address format, OTP/recovery/export and **0–499-MAU free-tier boundary** remain untested; `raider-pass-portal/` is an isolated, unconfigured preview that cannot mint. Publish anti-farming/distribution policy, independently review and deploy the hard-40-cap NFT with owner signing, fund issuer-gas sponsorship, audit separate claim consent/mint fulfilment and verify actual Monad mint ownership. Then publish the holder-raider rule, **pass-unverified-slot reservation/expiry** (distinct from indefinite **unaddressed** reservations), complete public email-account rules and exact UTC **start and end**, including truthful per-email fairness, age/location/lawfulness limits, support/appeals/privacy, unused pool and final-close deadline. Independent legal guidance is sensible; no approvals are represented as existing.
2. Build a **real mail sender** with delivery verification, abuse throttles, credentials outside the workspace, bounce/support handling and account-loss procedures. A GET of a magic link must not consume it automatically; production URLs must be HTTPS. Harden frontend/session/CSRF/log/referrer controls and rehearse theft/expiry/replay/rate-limit failures.
3. Build account-bound, fresh signed proof from the opening raider's wallet, check current `ownerOf` against the verified Raider Pass contract, and decide whether the payout address must be the holder wallet or needs a separate raider-controlled, signed wallet binding. Then **redesign and review** the incompatible draft vault so ownership is enforced **at actual CHOG transfer**, including ≥50 auto-payout and final settlement; close permissionless redemption/race/bypass paths. Independently check the replacement source and deployed Monad runtime, actual CHOG/Entropy addresses and financial invariants; securely operate owner/sponsor roles, callbacks, signed batch/proof reconciliation, deep reorg and failed-entropy recovery. The 25-CHOG worst case, **not** the 7.25-CHOG expected prize, must back each accepted opening.
4. Add encrypted, off-host, **email-versioned** journal and capacity snapshots with an independently held latest digest, append-only public receipt/checkpoint visibility and a tested automatic halt/recovery protocol. No email recovery service presently exists.
5. Verify sponsor address control and sufficient MON, then verify a **different** deployed vault address and fund that vault with **1,000 CHOG** only after secure final checks. The user confirmed no CHOG transfer; do not request keys or seeds. An earlier read-only oracle fee quote was **1.4 MON/request**, so 40 separate requests could require **56 MON plus gas**, before retries.
6. Demonstrate a complete disposable-asset rehearsal followed by confirmed mainnet funding/slots, exact published terms and healthy monitored service **before** changing `GAME_ONLY`. Never import browser sample passes or preview openings, or treat a claim-looking button as an on-chain NFT mint or token payment.
